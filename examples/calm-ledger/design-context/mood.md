# Mood — Calm Ledger

*Produced with mood-protocol v0.1 from the project moodboard, 2026-08-01. Provisional until the board is revisited after usability round one.*

## In three lines

A finance app that lowers your heart rate. The feeling of a tidy kitchen on a Sunday morning: everything in its place, nothing demanding attention. Quiet competence, warm but not chatty.

## Qualities

- **Steady** — nothing pulses, bounces, or urges. Motion is slow fades only.
- **Legible warmth** — warm paper neutrals, generous line height, ink instead of black.
- **Unhurried density** — show fewer numbers per screen than a finance app "should". White space is the trust signal.

## References

- Muji stationery: utility with softness.
- A well-set paperback page: the typography carries the calm.
- Japanese bank passbooks: money as a quiet record, not a dashboard.

## Anti-references

- NOT a trading terminal: no dense grids, no red/green semantics, no urgency.
- NOT a gamified fintech app: no streaks, no confetti, no mascot.
- NOT corporate dashboard: no KPI cards with big deltas and arrows.
