# Mood — Bright Start

*Produced with mood-protocol v0.1 from the project moodboard, 2026-06-12. Not revised since the July usability round. See decisions.md before applying the Rewarding and Kinetic qualities.*

## In three lines

Reading should feel like a party the child is throwing for themselves. Bright, loud, generous with praise. The opposite of the classroom: nobody is being assessed here, everybody is being cheered.

## Qualities

- **Exuberant** — saturated colour, big friendly shapes, nothing muted or tasteful.
- **Rewarding** — finishing something should be visibly, unmistakably celebrated.
- **Kinetic** — things bounce, pop, and wobble. Motion is part of the fun.

## References

- A birthday party tea table: paper hats, too much colour, joy without taste.
- Richard Scarry pages: dense, warm, endlessly re-readable.
- Playground equipment: primary colours, built to be climbed on.

## Anti-references

- NOT a classroom: no marking, no red pen, no sense of being tested.
- NOT a productivity app: no streaks framed as obligation, no guilt for a missed day.
- NOT a toy that patronises: children notice condescension faster than adults do.
